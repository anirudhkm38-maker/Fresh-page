# Fresh Page — Repeatable Reset, Managers Flavor, Ready to Deploy

## What it is, in two lines
The 3-minute reset becomes a tool you can run as many times a day as you need, in four flavors instead of three. After that, the site is fully verified and ready for the owner to press Deploy so the new flavors and the branded share card appear on the real domain.

## Who it's for
Anirudh (the owner, who deploys and shares the link) and stressed workers — including managers — who want a private, repeatable reset rather than a once-a-day ritual.

## Core features and experience
- **Always-open reset.** The three flavor tabs ("Just a reset", "Sunday night", "First week") stay visible on every screen of the tool, including the closing screen, so a visit can always start instantly. Picking a flavor at any point clears everything from memory (the nothing-stored promise holds) and opens step one of that flavor. The closing line stays as a full stop, not a wall; a small line under the closing text notes it can be run again anytime. Section copy changes from "it closes — on purpose" to reflect that it is welcome as often as needed.
- **Managers flavor.** A fourth tab, "For managers", with its own content in the same calm voice: chips for the manager's day (the 1:1 being avoided, a team running on fumes, delivering hard feedback, pressure from above, a hire that isn't working, just need a reset), a release prompt about the weight of being the one people lean on, two hands columns (what's mine to hold / what belongs to the team or the company), six original affirmations about leading calmly, a body nudge, and its own closing line.
- **Live deployment.** Nothing needs building for this: the owner presses Deploy in the Emergent dashboard, and the live domain then shows the share card (Open Graph / Twitter image), the new flavors, and the repeatable reset. The site has no database, so there is no data to migrate — deploy ships the site itself.
